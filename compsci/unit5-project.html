Unit 5: Impacts of Computing — Project

The Tech Impact Briefing

Built in Google Slides. 100 points.

Standards Addressed

Nevada Academic Content Standards for Computer Science & Integrated Technology: 9-12.IC.C.1 (impacts on personal/ethical/social/economic/cultural practices), 9-12.IC.C.2 (reduce bias and equity deficits), 9-12.IC.C.3 (algorithms applied across disciplines), 9-12.IC.C.4 (impacts of AI on society), 9-12.IC.SLE.1–3 (IP law, data privacy, and social/economic implications of privacy) — the same standards Chapter 11 (The Bias Test), Chapter 12 (The Cross-Discipline Brief, The Privacy Audit) and Chapter 13's AI content build toward.

Objective

Investigate one real, current technology's real impact on society — who it helps, who it could disadvantage, who owns it, and whether that tradeoff is worth it.

Deliverable: A Google Slides deck, minimum 7 slides. Turn in a PDF export or a text file with your share link below.

New to Google Slides? Start here: Ch 4.0, The Office Suite, From Zero »

Steps

Each step below links to the chapter section that actually teaches how to do it — do that first if you haven't already.

  1. Choose one real current technology (a social media algorithm, an AI writing/image tool, facial recognition, a wearable health tracker, a recommendation engine, etc.).
  2. Explain It — what it does and how it works, in plain language.
  3. Bias & Equity Test — describe a real or realistic test of who this technology could disadvantage, and what you'd find. How to: Ch 11.2, Testing for Bias »
  4. Cross-Disciplinary Connection — show how this same algorithm/technology shows up in a different field than the one it's best known for. How to: Ch 13.1, One Algorithm, Many Fields »
  5. Privacy & Data — what personal data does it collect, and what are the real privacy concerns? How to: Ch 12.2, Reading Your Own Footprint Honestly »
  6. IP & Legal Angle — who owns the data or the algorithm, and what's the tradeoff of that ownership?
  7. Your Verdict — is this technology a net positive or net negative for society? Defend it with real evidence, not just opinion. How to: Ch 13.2, AI's Impact on Society, Honestly »
