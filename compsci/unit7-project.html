Unit 7: Networks and the Internet — Project

The Network Audit

Built in Google Slides. 100 points.

Standards Addressed

Nevada Academic Content Standards for Computer Science & Integrated Technology: 9-12.NI.NCO.1 (scalability/reliability of networks — routers, switches, servers, topology, addressing), 9-12.NI.C.1 (sensitive data affected by malware/attacks), 9-12.NI.C.2 (recommend security measures), 9-12.NI.C.3 (usability vs. security tradeoffs), 9-12.NI.C.4 (tradeoffs when implementing cybersecurity recommendations) — the same standards Chapter 17 (The Network Map), Chapter 18 (The Threat Log) and Chapter 19 (The Tradeoff Matrix, The Security Brief) build toward.

Objective

Audit a network — map its topology, identify real threats it faces, and recommend security measures with the tradeoffs explicitly weighed, not just "the most secure option."

Deliverable: A Google Slides deck, minimum 6 slides. Turn in a PDF export or a text file with your share link below.

New to Google Slides? Start here: Ch 4.0, The Office Suite, From Zero »

Steps

Each step below links to the chapter section that actually teaches how to do it — do that first if you haven't already.

  1. Diagram a real or realistic network (a home network, a small business, or a generalized school-style network — not exact real infrastructure) showing routers, switches, servers, and how devices are addressed. How to: Ch 17.1, Routers, Switches & Topology »
  2. Identify 2+ real, specific threats this network is exposed to — not generic "hackers." How to: Ch 18.1, Malware Families » · Ch 18.3, Network Attacks & Real Defenses »
  3. For each threat, recommend a security measure, reasoning about efficiency and feasibility, not just picking the most secure option regardless of cost. How to: Ch 19.2, Writing a Recommendation Someone Will Fund »
  4. Compare 2 competing security measures head-to-head on usability vs. security tradeoffs. How to: Ch 19.1, The Usability »
  5. Final Recommendation — pick your overall approach and explicitly state what tradeoff you're accepting.
