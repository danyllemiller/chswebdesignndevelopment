Grading Policy

Standards-based assessment at Carson High School. Your grade reflects mastery — not just completion.

Assessment Overview

Grades provide feedback on your progress toward mastering professional web standards. We use the official Carson High School grading scale across all assessments — labs, projects, career readiness, and exams. Every category has a specific weight that reflects its importance to your development as a web professional.

Web Design I — Category Weights
Assignments & Skills 50%
Technical computer-based labs and written theory work. The largest category — daily quality of work matters most.
Projects & Quizzes 20%
Unit exams (written and practical), quizzes, and major project deliverables.
Semester Exams 20%
End-of-semester demonstrations of full-course mastery. Cumulative and performance-based.
Career Readiness 10%
Professionalism, time management, habit tracking, and participation. See breakdown below.
Web Design II, III & AS — Category Weights
Assignments & Skills 35%
High-level coding labs, implementation tasks, and technical skill demonstrations.
Projects & Quizzes 35%
Unit projects, client deliverables, research, and in-class agency work. Weight increases at this level because projects are the product.
Semester Exams 20%
Advanced technical applications and full-stack demonstrations of end-of-semester mastery.
Career Readiness 10%
Workplace professionalism, ethics, and the daily habits that define your professional reputation.

Career Readiness — What Counts

The 10% Career Readiness category isn't participation points. It reflects the habits that separate someone who can code from someone who can hold a job. Here's exactly what feeds into it:

Clock-In Punctuality

Use the Employee Portal (on My Grades) to clock in when class starts and out when it ends. On-time punches earn a $5 bonus on your bi-weekly simulated paycheck. Tardiness is tracked — just like a real job. Your attendance data directly feeds your Guild Score.

Daily Habit Tracker (My Planner)

My Planner on your student dashboard tracks daily habits — focus, effort, preparation, mindset. Completing your daily tracker consistently shows you're showing up mentally, not just physically. Habit data is averaged over the last 28 days and contributes 25% of your Guild Score.

Classroom Conduct & Professionalism

Respectful communication, following directions without reminders, helping classmates, and representing the studio well during client interactions. This is the part of the job description that says "team player."

Time Management & Self-Direction

Do you use work time to work? Do you start tasks without being told twice? Do you manage your own deadlines? At the advanced level this is especially critical — the in-class agency doesn't have a supervisor hovering over you.

Academic Integrity

Plagiarism means turning in someone else's work as your own or copying code without attribution. Both the giver and the taker receive a ZERO. Using AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot) is allowed for learning and assistance — submitting AI output as your own original work without understanding or attribution is not. If you use it, cite it and be able to explain every line.

The Fine Print
No Extra Credit. Grades reflect mastery. Do the assigned work well.
Redos Welcome. Redo any project until you're satisfied — within the current or immediately following unit.
Life Happens. Request extensions at least 24 hours in advance by email. Don't wait until the day of.
Late Penalty. Work submitted after the grace period is accepted for a maximum of 60% (D−).
