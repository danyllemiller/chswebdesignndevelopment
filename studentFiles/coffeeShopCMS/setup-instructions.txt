COFFEESHOP CMS — Chapter 12 Setup Instructions
===============================================

This project uses WordPress via LocalWP (not traditional HTML files).
There are no starter code files to download — follow these steps to begin.

STEP 1: Install LocalWP
-----------------------
Download LocalWP for free at: https://localwp.com
Install and open it on your computer.

STEP 2: Create Your Site
------------------------
1. Click the "+" button in LocalWP to create a new site.
2. Name it exactly: CoffeeShopCMS
3. Choose "Preferred" environment settings.
4. Set a username and password you will remember (you'll use these to log in).
5. Click "Add Site" and wait for LocalWP to finish setting it up.

STEP 3: Open WordPress Admin
-----------------------------
1. In LocalWP, click "Admin" or "WP Admin" to open the dashboard in your browser.
2. Log in with the username and password you just created.

STEP 4: You're Ready
---------------------
Your Chapter 12 walkthroughs begin from the WordPress dashboard.
Follow the chapter instructions to build your Local Legends Coffee Shop website.
