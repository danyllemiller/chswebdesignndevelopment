Partner Dots

Two pages of 1-inch dots: 4-Square (7 colors × 4 dots, letters A–G) and Pair-Share (14 colors × 2 dots, numbers 1–14).

  • Print in color, with Scale: 100% / Actual size and Headers and footers off. Turn on Background graphics if the dots print white.
  • Check one dot with a ruler: it should be exactly 1 inch across.
  • Print on full-page sticker paper to make stickers, or on cardstock and cut along the black circles.
