Anonymous Survey Results

Agency 360 / Comp Sci Course Review
Weekly Pulse
Privacy limits, plainly stated: no response row ever stores a timestamp, name, or device identifier — results only appear once at least 4 responses exist for a survey/term (3 for a Pulse week), and open-text answers are shown in a randomized order every time, never by submission order. A skipped item just isn't in the averages. Anyone with direct database access can still see raw rows below the threshold; that gate protects this page's view, not the underlying table.
