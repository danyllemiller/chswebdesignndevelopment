Teacher Guide: Give a Practice Test

Everything you need to start a session, get students in, give supports, and download your results.

The short version: go to wprpracticepro.com → Give a test, enter the teacher passcode, pick a test, and click Start session. Students join with your class code. Download your reports before you click End the test. Nothing is saved: 15 minutes after you end the test, everything is erased.

On this page: 1. Start a session · 2. Get students in · 3. During the test · 4. Download your reports · 5. End the test · Tips

1. Start a session

  1. Go to wprpracticepro.com and click Give a test (top right) or Give a practice test.
  2. Teacher passcode: type the passcode your school shared with you. It isn't posted on this site. If you don't have it, ask the teacher who shared WPR Practice Pro with you, or email contact@wprpracticepro.com.
  3. Your name: type your name. It only shows on the class report you download.
  4. Test length: pick one.
    OptionUse it for
    Full state practice: 100 questions, 1 hourA full dress rehearsal for the real test
    50 questions, 40 minutesA shorter practice test
    25 questions, 20 minutesA quick check
    10-minute Sprint: 100 questions, answer as many as you canWeekly warm-up practice (for example, every Monday). The score counts only the questions a student answered.
    Baseline CFA: the PLC's 50 questions, untimedThe common assessment. Every student gets the same 50 questions in their own order, with no time limit. These questions never appear on practice tests.
  5. Questions from: Standard WRS practice questions (most tests), Standard questions + question banks (bank questions make up to half the test), or Only question banks.
  6. Who can take this test?
    • Anyone with the class code: fastest. Students join with the code, their name, and their student ID.
    • Only the students on my class list: paste your list from a spreadsheet, one student per line: Last name, First name, Student ID. Only those students can join, and you can set extra time and Read aloud before anyone joins. The list is saved only in your browser on that computer so you can reuse it.
  7. Click Start session. Your session page shows a big class code and a join link with a Copy button.

2. Get students in

  1. Put the class code on the board, or paste the join link where students can click it.
  2. Students go to wprpracticepro.com → Join a test, and type the class code, their first and last name, and their student ID number. No student accounts.
  3. Each student clicks Start the test when ready. Their clock starts when they click it, so everyone's time is their own.
Tell students before they start: the test runs full screen. Switching tabs, opening another site or app, or translating the page locks the test with a warning and tells you. The second time, their test is submitted. If full screen turns off, the test hides until they go back to full screen.

3. During the test

Your session page updates on its own: who has joined, who is testing, how many they've answered, and who has finished.

Extra time

Click the student's time in the Time allowed column and pick Time and a half or Double time. You can change it before or during the test; their clock updates within a few seconds.

Read aloud

Click Add in the Supports column, check that the student has headphones (or is testing alone), then click Turn on. A Read aloud button appears on their test.

A break

In Supports, click Pause their clock for a break and wait until their screen says the test is paused. Click Restart their clock when they're back.

To set supports before anyone joins, start the session with Only the students on my class list. Downloads show the time allowed and the supports used, never why, and nothing on the student's screen says why.

4. Download your reports (before you end the test)

The download buttons turn on once students finish. Download what you need before clicking End the test.

ReportWhat it shows
Printable class reportThe easiest class-wide summary: class average, median, how many passed (75%+), a score chart, bars for every state standard and indicator running red (failing) to green (passing), the most-missed questions, and every student's score.
Where to reteachThe weakest indicators first, how many students scored under 75% on each, and the most-missed questions with the wrong answer students picked most.
All student reports (PDF)One page set per student: score, results by state standard (the same red-to-green bars), every missed question with their answer and the correct answer, and what each standard means. Students are named by initials only.
Score summary (CSV)One row per student: questions answered, correct, score %, accuracy %, times they left the test, how it ended, time allowed, and supports. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
Question-by-question (CSV)Every question every student saw: the question, their answer, the correct answer, and right or wrong.
By standard (CSV)One row per student with their percent on each state standard and indicator.

Saving the class report as a PDF: it opens in a new tab. Click Print or save as PDF, choose Save as PDF, and under More settings turn on Background graphics so the colored bars show.

5. End the test

  1. When everyone is done (or time is up), click End the test. It ends the test for everyone; anyone still testing is scored on the answers they saved.
  2. Students can then see their answers and save their own report for 15 minutes. Ask them to keep the test page open until you end it.
  3. After those 15 minutes, everything is erased, including your session page. Sessions also erase automatically after 12 hours.

Tips

  • Students see their score as soon as they finish, but not which questions they missed until you end the test.
  • Each student gets a different random set of questions on practice tests, so the most-missed list fills in with larger classes.
  • Question banks are shared with every teacher who has the passcode and saved for future tests. They hold questions only, never student information. Never upload real state test questions.
  • Keep the teacher passcode with teachers only. Anyone with it can start a session and add question banks.
