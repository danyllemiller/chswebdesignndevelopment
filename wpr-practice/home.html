Why it works

Made for how test prep actually works

Students do better on test day when the test screen, the timing, and the tricky questions are not new to them.

Feels like test day

One question per screen, lettered choices, Flag for Review, a question navigator, an on-screen calculator and scratch paper, and a countdown clock.

Full-screen lockdown

The test runs full screen. Leaving the tab, exiting full screen, or translating the page locks the test behind a warning; a second time submits it. You see every exit on your screen.

Every answer explained

When you end the test, students see every question, the right answer, and why. That includes the work-life questions the state test grades in ways students don't expect.

Extra time, student by student

Give any student time and a half or double time with one click, even after they've started. Their clock updates in seconds. Nothing on screen says why, and your downloads just say "Time allowed."

Answers when you're ready

Students see their score the moment they finish. The answers unlock only when you end the test, so they can't spread while classmates are still working.

A report students can share

Each student saves a PDF with their score, how they did on each topic, and every question they missed. It shows initials only, never a name or student ID.

How it works

From "open the site" to "test started" in one minute

Start a session

Go to /teacher, enter the teacher passcode, and pick a length: 25, 50, or 100 questions, or a 10-minute sprint.

Share the class code

You get a six-letter code and a link. Students type the code, their name, and their student ID. No sign-ups, no emails.

Watch, end, download

See who is testing, time left, and scores as they come in. When everyone is done, end the test: students get their answers and their report for 15 minutes, then everything is erased. Download your results before then.

Test security

Built so the practice is their own work

Practice only helps if students do the thinking. These protections are on for every test, with nothing to set up.

Full screen, exits caught

Switching tabs or apps, or leaving full screen, locks the test behind a warning and shows up on your dashboard. The second time, the test is submitted.

No translating

The state test is in English only, so the practice test is too. Browsers are told not to translate it, and if a translator runs anyway, the test locks.

No copy, paste, or right-click

Questions can't be copied out of the test, and nothing can be pasted in. The scratch paper is just for working things out.

Answers stay on the server

The correct answers never reach a student's browser until you end the test, so they can't be dug out of the page or shared while others are still testing.

A different test for each student

Every student gets their own random set of questions in a different order, so there's nothing to copy from a neighbor.

Private sessions

Only teachers with the passcode can start a test. Each session has a private key, so you see only your own class, and the class code stops working when you end the test.

Privacy

We don't keep student data. On purpose.

Student names and scores exist only in temporary memory while your test runs, and for 15 minutes after you end it. Then they're gone, for good.

  • Temporary memory only. Test results are never written to a database, a file, or a backup.
  • Erased automatically 15 minutes after you end the session (time for students to save their reports), after 12 hours, or if the server restarts.
  • Student reports use initials. The PDF a student saves never shows their name or student ID, so it's safe to share.
  • No accounts, ads, or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared, ever.
