Syllabus Quiz

Web Design II, III & AS | Instructor: Ms. Miller

10 questions on the grading policy, Career Readiness, and academic integrity. Answer every question, then check your score.

1. In Web Design II, III & AS, what percentage of your grade comes from Assignments?

Assignments are 35% of your grade — the same weight as Projects/Quizzes.

2. How much of your grade comes from Projects/Quizzes?

Projects/Quizzes are 35% of your grade, matching Assignments.

3. How much of your grade comes from the Final Exam, and how much from Career Readiness?

Assignments (35%) + Projects/Quizzes (35%) + Final Exam (20%) + Career Readiness (10%) = 100%.

4. How do you earn the $5 bonus on your bi-weekly simulated paycheck?

On-time clock-ins through the Employee Portal earn a $5 bonus on your bi-weekly simulated paycheck. Tardiness is tracked just like a real job.

5. Your Guild Score is a separate class ranking. What three signals build it, and at what weights?

Guild Score = Grades 50% + Habits (My Planner) 25% + Attendance (Clock-In) 25%.

6. How is your My Planner habit-tracker data calculated for the Guild Score?

Habit data from My Planner is averaged over the last 28 days and makes up 25% of your Guild Score.

7. You want to redo a project to improve your grade. What's the rule?

Redos are welcome — redo any project until you're satisfied, within the current or immediately following unit.

8. You know you'll need extra time on an assignment. What's the correct move?

Life happens — request extensions at least 24 hours in advance by email. Don't wait until the day of. Work turned in late without an extension is capped at 60% (D-).

9. What's true about using AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot on your work?

Using AI tools is allowed for learning and assistance. Submitting AI output as your own original work without understanding or attribution is not — if you use it, cite it and be able to explain every line.

10. At which tardy does your teacher first contact home about it?

Every student is marked absent by default until a Tardy Form is turned in. The first call home comes at the third tardy.
