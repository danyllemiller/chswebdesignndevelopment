Capstone Description

This course transitions students from front-end designers into full-stack engineers. The focus shifts from "how to build a page" to "how to build a system." Students connect applications to live databases, consume third-party APIs, and ship real products to real users using professional tools: Git, GitHub, Figma, WordPress, and cloud hosting.

Advanced Studies (AS) students operate as a working in-class agency — taking on community and school projects with actual clients, stakeholders, and deadlines. Your code runs in production.

The In-Class Agency

You Are the Studio

Advanced students don't just do assignments — they run an agency. You take on real community projects, set your own timelines, and deliver to stakeholders outside this classroom.
