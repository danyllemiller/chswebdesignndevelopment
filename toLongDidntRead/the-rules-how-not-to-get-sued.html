TL;DR: Chapter 3

1. Intellectual Property (IP)

Copyright: Automatic creative shield. Trademark: Brand identity (Nike). Patent: Invention protection (lasts 20 years). Trade Secret: Indefinite protection for code logic.

2. Security (HTTPS & SSL)

HTTP is a Postcard. HTTPS is a Sealed Letter. It uses an 8-step handshake and an SSL/TLS certificate to encrypt data.

3. Privacy Laws

GDPR: EU law, Opt-In model, Right to be Forgotten. SB220: Nevada law, Opt-Out model for data sales.

4. Accessibility (a11y)

Follow POUR principles for 4 disability types — Visual, Auditory, Motor, Cognitive. Target: WCAG Level AA. Audit for Alt Text, Color Contrast (4.5:1), and Keyboard access. It's an ADA civil right.

5. Citing Third-Party Work

"I gave credit" ≠ "I had permission." A real citation needs the creator's name, the license (linked), and the source URL. No visible license = All Rights Reserved.

6. Global Privacy Patchwork

Beyond GDPR & SB220: CCPA/CPRA (California), COPPA (under-13 data), HIPAA (health data), CAN-SPAM (email). Most non-US laws (UK, Canada, Brazil, Japan, China, India...) are Opt-In. Build for the strictest law — Privacy by Design.

ETHICS ALERT: Avoid Dark Patterns (tricking users) and Algorithmic Bias (prejudice in AI datasets).
