Syllabus Quiz

Computer Science Foundations | Instructor: Ms. Miller

10 questions on the grading policy, attendance rules, and academic integrity. Answer every question, then check your score.

1. In CS, what percentage of your grade comes from Assignments?

Assignments are 60% of your grade, Tests/Quizzes 20%, and the Final Exam 20%.

2. How much of your grade comes from Tests/Quizzes?

Tests/Quizzes are 20% of your grade — the same weight as the Final Exam.

3. How much of your grade comes from the Final Exam?

Assignments (60%) + Tests/Quizzes (20%) + Final Exam (20%) = 100%.

4. Each of the 7 units ends with a unit test. What score on that test excuses you from that unit's assignments?

80% or better on a unit test excuses you from that unit's assignments — you proved you already know it.

5. If you score 75-79% on a unit test, what happens?

75-79% earns you one retest, with a reteach day built in first. Score 80% on the retest and you're excused from that unit's work.

6. You test out of Unit 1 with an 80%+ score. Do you still have to take the Unit 2 test?

Testing out earns you a break from that unit's assignments, not from the next unit's test. To test out of the whole class you have to test out of every unit — there's no single exam that skips the semester.

7. How soon after the bell must you clock in, or you're considered late?

Even though the coursework is self-paced, clock-ins are required every day within the first 5 minutes of class.

8. You arrive late to class. What do you need to do to make sure it's marked as a tardy and not an absence?

Every student is marked absent by default. If the Tardy Form isn't turned in, the absence stands — the form is what protects your record.

9. At which tardy does your teacher first contact home about it?

1st = private check-in, 2nd = written reflection, 3rd = home contact + support plan. Tardies reset each grading period.

10. What happens if you copy someone else's code and turn it in as your own?

Both the giver and the taker receive a zero — copying code without attribution counts as plagiarism either way.
