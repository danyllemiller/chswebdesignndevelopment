How It Works
Step-by-step guides
New to the platform? Start here. Each guide walks you through one tool from start to finish.
1
Setting Up Your Account
Your teacher adds you to the roster before the first day. You register and set your own password on your first visit to /login.html.
1
On the login page, click First time? Register here. Enter your student ID, first name, and last name exactly as they appear on your schedule.
2
Choose a username (letters and numbers only) and a password that is at least 8 characters. Write these down — you will need them every day.
3
After registering, log in normally with your new username and password. You land on the home dashboard.
4
If you forget your password, click Forgot password? on the login page. Enter your student ID and username — your password resets to your student ID, and you are prompted to set a new one immediately.
5
Your account works on any device — phone, Chromebook, or desktop. Your planner, notebook, and grades sync automatically.
If registration fails: Your student ID may not be in the system yet. Let your teacher know and they will add you from the Roster page — usually takes less than a minute.
2
The School Calendar
Navigate to Calendar in the top nav. Four views are available at the top of the page — pick whichever works for how you think about time.
1
Month view shows the full month with color-coded day types. A-days and B-days are labeled so you know which classes meet.
2
Week view shows a 5-day block with period times. Use Previous / Next to move between weeks.
3
Day view shows a single day with all periods and the day type (A-day, B-day, minimum day, etc.).
4
Events view lists upcoming dates — useful for seeing test days, minimum days, and breaks at a glance.

3
My Progress Report
Navigate to My Grades in the top nav. This is a live view of your teacher's gradebook — your scores only, no one else's.
1
The summary cards at the top show your current overall grade, how many assignments you have completed out of the total, and your points earned.
2
The gradebook table below lists every assignment with your score, the maximum points, and the percentage. Missing scores show as blank.
3
The Achievements section shows any badges you have earned — attendance, perfect scores, on-time submissions, and others.
4
The Proficiency Growth chart at the bottom plots your self-assessment scores over time. It shows whether your confidence and skill are trending up, and your teacher uses it to spot where to give extra help.

4
My Notebook
Navigate to My Notebook in the top nav. This is your digital binder — every entry you write is saved to the server and accessible from any device.
1
Select the chapter you are working on from the chapter picker at the top. Entries are organized by chapter so you can find them later.
2
Click New Entry and choose the entry type: Notes, Do Now, Exit Ticket, or Worksheet. Give it a title and write your entry.
3
Click Save Entry. The entry appears in the list below, timestamped with the time you submitted it. Your teacher can see this timestamp — it shows you completed the work during class.
4
To edit an entry, click the pencil icon next to it. Changes save automatically when you click Save.
5
Use the category filter tabs (Notes / Do Now / Exit Ticket / Worksheet) to quickly find the type of entry you are looking for across all chapters.
Do Nows and Exit Tickets are graded on completion, not correctness. Write your honest answer — the timestamp shows you were present and engaged, and that is what counts.
5
The Student Planner
Navigate to My Planner in the top nav. Six tabs organize everything: Grades, Habits, Goals, To-Do, Journal, and Calendar. Your data is saved on the server and syncs across all your devices.
1
First-time setup — Schedule: Click the settings gear at the top right. Under Schedule, enter the class name for each of your 8 periods (A1, A3, A5, A7 on A-days; B2, B4, B6, B8 on B-days). This pre-fills every grade log entry so you never have to re-type your class names.
2
Grades tab: All 8 of your periods appear as cards (2 per row). Each card shows the period, your class name, overall grade percentage, and letter grade. Click + Add on any card to log a grade for that class — the period is already selected, so just enter the assignment name, score, and date.
3
Weighted categories: Click the gear icon (⚙) on any period card to define grade categories — for example: Homework 20%, Quizzes 25%, Tests 40%, Projects 15%. When you tag each grade entry to a category, the planner switches from a simple average to a weighted average automatically.
4
Habits tab: Add habits you want to build (one check-in per day). A 28-day heat map shows your consistency at a glance — darker squares mean more days completed. Tap the suggestion chips for common school habits to add them with one click.
5
Goals tab: Set goals by cadence — daily micro-goals, weekly goals, marking period goals, and year-end goals. An achievement dashboard tracks how many you complete. Suggested school goals are available as one-click chips.
6
To-Do & Calendar tabs: Add assignments and due dates to your to-do list, then view them on the planner calendar. The calendar shows A-days and B-days synced to the school schedule — you always know which classes meet on which day.
7
Journal tab: Write daily reflections. Entries are private — only you can see them. Use it to track how class is going, note things to ask about, or just decompress.
Your teacher can view your planner in read-only mode. They can see your logged grades, habits, and goals — but cannot edit anything. This is so they can spot if you are struggling and offer help before grades slip.

6
My Employee Portal
Navigate to My Grades — the Employee Portal appears at the top of that page. Your portal shows your role, hourly rate, shift history, and a paystub generator organized by bi-weekly pay periods.
1
Clocking in: At the start of class, answer the warm-up question on your home page. Submitting the answer records your clock-in timestamp automatically — you do not click a separate button.
2
Clocking out works the same way at the end of class. Your total time for that shift is calculated when both punches are recorded.
3
In the Shift Logs table, each row shows the date, clock-in and clock-out times, on-time status, total hours logged, and estimated gross earnings for that shift.
4
Use the Previous / Next arrows at the top of the portal to browse earlier pay periods. Each period is 14 days. The current period is shown by default.
5
Click Generate Paystub to open your earnings statement for the selected pay period. It shows regular pay, performance bonuses (earned for on-time clock-ins and clock-outs), tax withholdings, and net pay.
On-Time Bonus: Each on-time clock-in or clock-out earns you a $5.00 performance bonus on top of your hourly rate — that's up to $10 extra per class day. Being punctual literally pays.

Exams & Quizzes
Your teacher will tell you when to start an exam and give you the link. Scores post automatically when you submit — no waiting for a teacher to enter them.
1
Open the exam link your teacher provides. The exam is tied to a specific chapter — make sure you are on the right one before you start.
2
Answer each question and click Submit when you are finished. Do not close the tab mid-exam — your progress is not auto-saved until you submit.
3
After submitting, your score appears on-screen immediately. The score also posts to your My Progress Report page so you can track it alongside your other grades.
4
If something goes wrong during the exam (page crash, network drop), let your teacher know right away. They can see submission records on the admin side and reset your attempt if needed.
Pre-assessments vs. Chapter Exams: Pre-assessments at the start of a unit measure what you already know — they do not count against your grade. Chapter and summative exams are graded. Your teacher will make clear which is which before you start.
