What's Included
Built for the way you actually teach
Everything was designed around the real daily rhythm of a classroom — not a generic learning management system.

School Calendar

See A-days, B-days, minimum days, and breaks at a glance in Day, Week, Month, or Events view. Upload your district calendar once and it's live for every device.

Teacher Task Checklist

A contextual task list that changes as you switch views. Daily tasks today, weekly tasks this week, monthly tasks this month — and it automatically hides during breaks.

Live Gradebook

Enter grades inline, update an entire column at once, and view each student's running average. Organized by course and section.

Student Notebooks

Students write notes, Do Nows, and Exit Tickets directly in the platform. You can browse every student's entries by chapter from your teacher view.

Roster & Sections

Upload your class roster via CSV, create sections, assign students, reset passwords, and archive past years — all without touching a database.

Payroll & Timesheets

Students clock in with a warm-up answer each day. You get a full timesheet report by student and by period for payroll processing.

Exam & Quiz Engine

Question banks tied to chapter numbers. Students take exams, scores post automatically, and you see per-question breakdowns to identify gaps.

Bell Schedule

The home dashboard always shows the current period with a live countdown — students know exactly where they should be.

Secure Login

Every student has a personal login. Passwords are hashed with industry-standard bcrypt. Students can self-service reset with their ID if they get locked out.

Student Planner

A personal academic planner for each student — grade log organized by period, habit tracker with heat map, goals dashboard, to-do list, journal, and a school calendar. You can view any student's planner in read-only mode from your teacher view.

How It Works
Step-by-step walkthroughs
You don't need to configure anything to get started. Here's how to use each major feature.
1
Using the School Calendar
Navigate to Calendar in the top nav. Four views are available at the top of the page.
1
Month view shows the full month with color-coded day types. Click any day to jump to that day's detail.
2
Week view shows the 5-day block with period times. Use the Previous / Next arrows to move between weeks.
3
Day view shows a single day with all periods and the day type (A-day, B-day, minimum day, etc.).
4
Events view is a filterable list of upcoming dates — good for sharing with students or parents.
5
To update the calendar (new school year, schedule changes): go to the Admin settings page, upload your CSV file in YYYY-MM-DD,TYPE,Description format, and it updates immediately for everyone.

2
Your Teacher Checklist
The checklist appears automatically below the calendar when you're logged in as a teacher. It adjusts based on your current view.
1
In Day view, you see today's task list. Check items off as you complete them throughout the day.
2
In Week view, you see this week's recurring tasks. The badge counter (e.g. "3/7") shows your progress.
3
In Month view, you see monthly tasks. If the displayed month contains a quarter-end or semester-end, quarterly and semester checklists appear automatically below.
4
During school breaks — any week where Monday through Friday are all non-school days — the checklist panel automatically hides. No action needed.
5
Use Mark All to check everything at once, or Reset to start the period fresh.
Teacher Work Days: Days marked as "Teacher Work Day," "Teacher Training," or "Professional Learning" in the calendar still show the checklist — because you're at school, even if students aren't.
3
Managing Your Gradebook
Navigate to Gradebook from the admin dashboard. Grades live in the database — nothing to export or upload.
1
Select a section from the dropdown. All students and assignments for that section load automatically.
2
Click any cell to edit a grade inline. Press Enter or click away to save.
3
To grade the whole class on one assignment at once, use the column header menu → "Batch Update." Enter a score once; click each student who earned it.
4
Students see only their own grades when they log in — they can't see classmates' scores.
4
Setting Up Your Roster
Go to Admin → Roster at the start of each semester to load your class.
1
Create your sections first (Admin → Sections). One section per class period.
2
Upload a CSV with your student roster. Required columns: student_id, first_name, last_name, section_id. Students are added or updated — existing records are not overwritten.
3
Students register on first login at the login page — they set their own username and password using their student ID to verify.
4
If a student forgets their password, they can self-reset with their name, ID, and username. Or you can reset it from the Roster page in one click — their ID becomes the temporary password, and they're prompted to change it immediately.
5
At the end of the year, use Archive Year to preserve records and clear the active roster for the incoming class.
5
Timesheets & Payroll
Students clock in at the start of class by answering a warm-up question. You get a complete record for payroll.
1
Students see a warm-up prompt on their home page at the start of each period. Answering it records their clock-in time.
2
Go to Admin → Payroll to view timesheets. Filter by student or by date range.
3
The Period Report tab shows total hours per student for a pay period, ready for payroll submission.
Tip: Each student's role (e.g. Junior Designer, Developer) carries a different hourly rate defined in the Pay Roles table. Update rates in Admin → Payroll before running period reports.
6
Reading Student Notebooks
Navigate to Admin → Notebooks to see every student's written work.
1
Filter by section, student, or chapter to narrow the view.
2
Entry categories — Notes, Do Now, Exit Ticket, Worksheet — are displayed as labeled tabs so you can quickly find what you're grading.
3
Each entry shows the timestamp, so you can verify students completed work during class time.
7
The Student Planner
Students access their planner at Student Tools → My Planner. You can view any student's planner in read-only mode from the teacher dashboard.
1
First-time setup — Schedule: The student opens the Schedule settings and enters their class name for each of the 8 periods (A1, A3, A5, A7 on A-days; B2, B4, B6, B8 on B-days). These period names pre-fill every grade log entry automatically.
2
My Grades tab: All 8 periods appear as cards (2 per row). Each card shows the period label, class name, overall grade percentage, and letter grade in the header. Students click the + Add button on a specific period card to log a grade — the period is pre-selected, so they only need to enter the assignment, score, and date.
3
Weighted grade categories: The gear icon (⚙) on each period card opens a category editor. Students (or you) can define categories — e.g. Homework 20%, Quizzes 25%, Tests 40%, Projects 15% — and set the weights. When grades are tagged to a category, the period grade switches from a simple average to a weighted average automatically.
4
Habits tab: Students add habits to track (up to one per day). A 28-day color heat map shows streak and consistency at a glance — darker squares mean more days completed. Suggested school habits (homework, reading, studying, etc.) are available as one-click chips.
5
Goals tab: Students set goals by cadence — daily micro-goals, weekly goals, marking period goals, and academic year goals. An achievement dashboard tracks completion. Suggested school-related goals are available as one-click chips.
6
To-Do & Calendar tabs: Students manage assignments and deadlines in the to-do list. The planner calendar shows A-days and B-days synced to the school schedule so students always know which classes meet on which day.
7
Viewing a student's planner as a teacher: From the Roster or gradebook, click the planner icon next to a student's name. The planner opens in read-only mode — you can see their grades, habits, goals, and to-dos but cannot edit anything.
Grade categories are saved per student: Each student's weighted category setup (and all their grade entries) is stored on the server and persists across logins and devices. Changes you see in the teacher read-only view are always current.
